# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

english.now is a project for language learners from every country and background. We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or English proficiency.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being patient with people who are still learning English or new to open source
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for learners and the community
- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, including mocking someone's language skills
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome advances
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Report unacceptable behavior to **hello@english.now**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
